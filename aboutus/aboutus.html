Get to Know Us

Our leadership is anchored by Dr. Sanjay Kumar Dixit, our Managing Director and a distinguished medical professional with MBBS and MD credentials. With a profound understanding of medicine and a deep-seated empathy for humanity, Dr. Dixit has guided the company’s strategic vision since his appointment as Managing Director on July 11, 2016. Supported by Director Reena Dixit, who has been a pillar of the company since its inception in 2014, our leadership team ensures that we remain at the forefront of pharmaceutical innovation.

Ethojen Lifesciences specializes in a diverse and extensive product portfolio designed to meet the evolving needs of healthcare. Our range includes high-quality Tablets (both Beta-lactam and non-beta lactam), Nutraceutical Capsules, Calcium Tablets, and Protein Powders. Every product we bring to market is a testament to our core philosophy: quality assurance is a keyword we never compromise with. By blending pharmaceutical expertise with a genuine connection to patient welfare, we continue to set benchmarks for excellence and reliability in the industry.

Our Vision

We aim to be a leading pharmaceutical company recognized in India and abroad for introducing good quality products, which shall be affordable and very useful for the community. We also aspire to give the best value to our stakeholders through our quality and consumer-oriented services and innovative products.

Our Mission

We are highly dedicated and committed to serving the humanity. Along with time, the trends, generation and thinking are changing. We are enthusiastic people with new thoughts and ideas and self motivated spirits. We would like to put our attention towards medical research while coming up with products, which would cause a change in the pharmaceutical industry. We are passionate about taking a leadership role to bring some positive changes in society and industry. We well understand what it means to be excellent in taking care of people who need us at the time they are helpless and ill. We shall overcome all the hurdles between a good health and happy life.

Management

We are a set of self motivated, qualified, healthy people with diversified thinking. Our team is highly focused on bringing in the best medicines for people by adapting the high quality based and goal oriented operations and practices. We are making ongoing efforts to improve the community health.
