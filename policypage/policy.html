Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Ethojen Lifesciences. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website.

1. No Data Collection

This website is purely informational. We do not use contact forms, user accounts, or login systems. Consequently, we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. No Cookies or Tracking

We do not use cookies, pixels, or any third-party tracking scripts (like Google Analytics) to monitor your behavior. Your visit to our site remains completely private.

3. External Interaction

If you choose to contact us via the email addresses provided on this site, your email and any information provided therein will only be used to respond to your specific inquiry and will not be shared with third parties.

4. Updates

This policy is effective as of May 2026. Any future changes to our privacy practices will be updated on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ethojenlifesciences.ltd@gmail.com.
