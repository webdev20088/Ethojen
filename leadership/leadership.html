Leadership

Reena Dixit

Director, Ethojen Lifesciences

Reena Dixit serves as a Director at Ethojen Lifesciences Private Limited, bringing a steadfast commitment to corporate governance and strategic growth. Registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (DIN: 07042605), she plays a pivotal role in overseeing the company’s administrative excellence and operational integrity. With a keen focus on organizational development, Reena ensures that the company remains aligned with its core values of transparency and professional ethics. Her leadership is instrumental in fostering a culture of accountability, driving the mission of Ethojen Lifesciences to provide accessible healthcare while upholding the rigorous regulatory standards of the pharmaceutical industry.

Dr. Sanjay Kumar Dixit

Managing Director, Ethojen Lifesciences

Dr. Sanjay Kumar Dixit is the Managing Director of Ethojen Lifesciences Private Limited. Holding MBBS and MD qualifications, he brings extensive medical knowledge and years of professional experience in healthcare and pharmaceutical services. Dr. Dixit is widely recognized for his compassionate approach, strong ethical values, and commitment to improving patient well-being. Under his leadership, the company has focused on innovation, quality, and trusted healthcare solutions. His vision combines medical excellence with humanity, helping build a healthcare organization dedicated to delivering reliable and affordable pharmaceutical products while maintaining the highest standards of care and integrity.
